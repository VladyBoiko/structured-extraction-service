# Business customers

Effective 2025-02-01.

## Business account

Companies and sole proprietors can open a business account. You need the company registration number and, in the EU, a VAT ID. Verification takes up to 2 business days.

## Invoices and VAT

Every order from a business account gets an invoice with VAT shown separately. In the EU, deliveries to another member state with a valid VAT ID are invoiced under the reverse charge rule.

## Payment terms

Verified companies can pay by bank transfer within 30 days of the invoice date (net 30). The credit limit is set individually after the first three orders.

## Volume discounts

| Quantity of the same item | Discount |
|---|---|
| 10–49 units | 5% |
| 50 units or more | individual offer |

Volume discounts do not combine with the loyalty programme.

## Account manager

Business accounts with more than 10,000 EUR or 400,000 UAH of orders per year get a personal account manager.

## Returns for business customers

The statutory right of withdrawal does not apply to business customers. Returns of unopened items within 14 days are possible by agreement.
