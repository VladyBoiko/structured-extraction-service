# Frequently asked questions

Updated: 2025-07-01.

## Do you have physical stores?

No. ElectroStore is an online store. There is no click-and-collect and no showroom.

## How do I contact support?

Use the chat on the website (daily 8:00–22:00 Kyiv time) or email support@electrostore.example. Phone support is available in Germany and Austria on business days from 9:00 to 18:00 CET.

## How fast do you reply?

Chat replies usually come within 5 minutes during working hours. Emails are answered within 1 business day.

## Can I order from the United Kingdom?

No. We ship only to Ukraine, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, Czechia and Slovakia.

## I received an email about a prize or crypto bonus. Is it from you?

No. ElectroStore does not run prize draws by email and never sends crypto bonuses. Do not click the link. See "Account and security".

## Can I buy an item that is out of stock?

Use "Notify me" on the product page. Some items can be pre-ordered; pre-orders can be cancelled any time before shipment.

## Do you sell refurbished devices?

No. We sell only new devices from authorised distributors.

## Does the price include VAT?

Yes. All prices for private customers include VAT. Business customers can get a VAT invoice.
