# ElectroStore Club loyalty programme

Version 2025. Effective 2025-04-01. Replaces the 2024 version.

## Status levels

| Status | Discount | Spend in the last 12 months |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 3% | from 15,000 UAH or 450 EUR |
| Gold | 7% | from 50,000 UAH or 1,500 EUR |
| Platinum | 10% | from 120,000 UAH or 3,500 EUR |

Members who had Gold or Platinum status on 31 March 2025 keep their status until their next review, but with the new discount rates.

## How the discount works

The discount is applied automatically at checkout when you are logged in to your account.

The club discount does not apply to:

- items that are already on sale;
- orders with a promo code (the larger of the two discounts is used);
- gift cards and Care+ plans.

## Status review

Status is recalculated every month based on your spend in the last 12 months. Returned orders do not count.

## Discount missing at checkout

Check that you are logged in and that the cart has no sale items. If the discount is still missing, do not pay: contact support before placing the order. A club discount cannot be added after payment.
