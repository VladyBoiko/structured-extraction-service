# ElectroStore Club loyalty programme

Version 2024. Effective 2024-01-01.

## Status levels

| Status | Discount | Annual spend required |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | 3% | from 15,000 UAH or 450 EUR |
| Gold | 10% | from 40,000 UAH or 1,200 EUR |
| Platinum | 12% | from 100,000 UAH or 3,000 EUR |

## How the discount works

The discount is applied automatically at checkout when you are logged in. It can be combined with sale prices and promo codes.

## Status review

Your status is reviewed once a year, on the anniversary of joining the programme.
