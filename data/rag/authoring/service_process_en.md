# How repairs work

Effective 2025-03-01. Applies to Ukraine, Germany and Austria.

## 1. Create a repair request

Open the order in your account and choose "Request repair". Describe the problem, when it started and what you have already tried. Attach photos or a video if the fault is visible.

## 2. Pickup

A courier collects the device for free within 2 business days. Pack the device in its original box if you still have it. Remove SIM cards, memory cards and cases.

## 3. Back up your data

Back up your data before sending a phone, tablet or laptop. Repairs may require a factory reset. ElectroStore and service centres are not responsible for lost data.

## 4. Diagnosis

Diagnosis takes up to 5 business days. You get an email with the result:

- warranty case: the repair is free and starts immediately;
- not a warranty case: we send a cost estimate and you decide whether to repair.

If you decline a paid repair, the diagnosis fee is 400 UAH in Ukraine or 25 EUR in Germany and Austria. The fee is waived if you accept the repair.

## 5. Repair and return

Warranty repairs are completed within 14 days in Ukraine. In Germany and Austria we aim for 10 business days. The repaired device is returned by courier. You can follow the status in your account.

## Software problems

Before sending a device in, try a software update and a restart. Many connection problems, such as Bluetooth drops after an update, are fixed by a firmware or driver update from the manufacturer.
