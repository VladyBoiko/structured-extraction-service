Validity

A gift card is valid for 3 years from the date of purchase. The remaining balance stays on the card until it expires.

Using a gift card

Enter the card code at checkout. You can use up to 3 gift cards in one order and pay the rest by any other method. A card bought for Ukraine can only be used in the Ukrainian store, and a EUR card only in the German and Austrian store.

Restrictions

  • gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash;
  • gift cards cannot be returned;
  • gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards;
  • the club discount does not apply to gift cards.

Lost card

We can block and reissue an electronic gift card if you provide the order number of the gift card purchase. A lost printed card without the purchase order number cannot be restored.
