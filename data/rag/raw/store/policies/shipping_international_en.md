# International shipping

Effective 2025-02-01.

## Countries we ship to

We ship to Ukraine, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, Czechia and Slovakia. We do not ship to the United Kingdom, the United States or any other country outside this list.

## Poland, Czechia and Slovakia

| Service | Price | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| DHL standard | 9.90 EUR | 4–8 business days |
| free shipping | from 150 EUR | 4–8 business days |

Large appliances are not shipped to Poland, Czechia or Slovakia.

## Tracking numbers

Every parcel gets a tracking number from the carrier:

- Nova Poshta (Ukraine): 14 digits, usually starting with 20 or 59;
- DHL: 10 to 20 characters, for example JJD followed by digits.

The order number in the format ES-12345 is not a tracking number. Carriers cannot find a parcel by the order number.

## Customs

Shipments inside the EU have no customs duties. Shipments to Switzerland and Ukraine may be subject to import VAT and duties, which the recipient pays.

## Delivery address changes

The delivery address can be changed only before the parcel is handed over to the carrier. See "Changing an order".
