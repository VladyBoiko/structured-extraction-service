# Payment methods

Effective 2025-01-15.

## Accepted methods

| Method | Ukraine | Germany / Austria | Switzerland |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visa, Mastercard | yes | yes | yes |
| Apple Pay, Google Pay | yes | yes | yes |
| PayPal | no | yes | yes |
| Klarna invoice (pay in 30 days) | no | yes | no |
| cash on delivery | yes, see the Ukrainian payment page | no | no |
| instalments | yes, see the Ukrainian payment page | no | no |

## "Payment pending" status

When you pay by card, the bank first places a hold on the amount. The order shows "Payment pending" until the bank confirms the payment. This usually takes a few minutes but can take up to 48 hours.

If the money has left your account and the status has not changed after 48 hours, contact support with the order number. Do not pay a second time.

## Cancelled orders and held amounts

If an order is cancelled before the payment is confirmed, the bank releases the hold automatically within 3–5 business days. ElectroStore cannot speed up this release.

## Currency

Orders in Ukraine are charged in UAH. Orders in Germany, Austria and Switzerland are charged in EUR. Your bank may add its own conversion fee.

## Security

We never ask for your card number, CVV code or one-time passwords by phone, email or chat.
