Account and security

Effective 2025-01-15.

Resetting your password

Choose "Forgot password" on the login page. We send a reset link to your email. The link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. If the email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.

Two-factor authentication

You can turn on two-factor authentication in Account settings → Security. We support authenticator apps only. SMS codes are not offered.

If you lose access to the authenticator app, use one of the 10 backup codes shown when you turned on two-factor authentication. Without a backup code, support verifies your identity by an order number and the last four digits of the payment card, and resets two-factor authentication within 2 business days.

Suspicious activity

If you see an order you did not place, change your password immediately and contact support. We freeze the account and cancel unshipped orders.

Phishing

ElectroStore never asks for your password, card number, CVV code or one-time codes. Messages offering prizes, crypto bonuses or "account verification" links are not from us. Forward them to abuse@electrostore.example.

Deleting your account

You can request account deletion in Account settings → Privacy. The account is deleted within 30 days. Invoices are kept as long as tax law requires, even after deletion.
