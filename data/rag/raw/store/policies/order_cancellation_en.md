# Cancelling an order

Effective 2025-01-15.

## Before shipment

You can cancel an order free of charge until its status changes to "Shipped". Open the order in your account and choose "Cancel order", or contact support with the order number.

If you have already paid, the refund is made to the same payment method within 3 business days after cancellation. A card hold that was never charged is released by your bank within 3–5 business days.

## After shipment

A shipped order can no longer be cancelled. You have two options:

1. Refuse the parcel when it arrives. In Ukraine the return delivery fee of 80 UAH is deducted from the refund. In Germany and Austria refusing the parcel is free.
2. Accept the parcel and return the item under the return policy of your region.

## Pre-orders

Pre-orders can be cancelled at any time before shipment without a fee.

## Cancellation by ElectroStore

We may cancel an order if the item is out of stock or the price on the website was clearly wrong. In that case you receive a full refund and an email with the reason.
