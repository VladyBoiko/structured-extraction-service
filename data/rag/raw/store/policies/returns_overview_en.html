Returns at a glance

Last updated: 2025-07-01. This page summarises the ElectroStore return rules for every country we ship to. The regional policy always takes precedence over this summary.

What can be returned

Items must be complete, undamaged and in the original packaging. Opened in-ear headphones, opened software and games, gift cards and made-to-order items cannot be returned.

How to start a return

Open the order in your account and choose "Return item", or contact support with your order number (format ES-12345). You will receive a return code or a return label by email.

Refund timing

Ukraine: within 7 days after the warehouse checks the item. Germany, Austria and Switzerland: within 14 days after we receive your withdrawal notice. Other countries: within 14 days after the item arrives at our warehouse.

Defective or damaged items

A return is for items you no longer want. If the item is faulty, use the warranty process. If it arrived damaged, follow the damaged delivery rules and report it within 48 hours.
