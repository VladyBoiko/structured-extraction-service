ElectroStore Care+ extended protection

Version 2025. Available in Ukraine, Germany and Austria.

What Care+ is

Care+ is an optional protection plan sold by ElectroStore. It extends the warranty by 2 years after the regular warranty ends and adds cover for accidental damage.

What is covered

  • manufacturing defects during the extra 2 years;
  • accidental damage such as a cracked screen, a drop or liquid damage: one claim per 12 months;
  • free pickup and return of the device.

For accidental damage claims you pay a deductible of 20% of the repair cost. Defect repairs have no deductible.

Price

Care+ costs 10% of the product price, paid once. It is available for products from 3,000 UAH in Ukraine or from 100 EUR in Germany and Austria.

When you can buy it

You can add Care+ at checkout or within 30 days after delivery from your account. After 30 days it can no longer be added.

Not covered

  • theft or loss;
  • cosmetic damage that does not affect function;
  • damage on purpose;
  • in-ear headphones and accessories under the price threshold.

Making a claim

Open the order in your account and choose "Care+ claim". Describe what happened and attach photos. A courier collects the device within 2 business days.
