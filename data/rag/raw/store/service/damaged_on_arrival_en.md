# Damaged or faulty on arrival

Effective 2025-03-01.

## Check the parcel at pickup

Inspect the parcel when you collect it. If the box is visibly damaged, open it in front of the carrier's staff. In Ukraine, ask Nova Poshta to write a damage report; in Germany and Austria, note the damage on the DHL delivery record.

## Hidden damage

If the damage is only visible after unpacking, report it within 48 hours of delivery. Send:

- the order number;
- photos of the item, the box and the shipping label;
- a short description of the damage.

Do not throw away the packaging until the case is closed.

## What we offer

For an item damaged in transit we send a replacement at our cost or refund the full amount, including shipping. You choose which.

## Dead on arrival

If a new device does not work at all or has a clear fault within 14 days of delivery, we treat it as dead on arrival (DOA). A DOA device is replaced with a new one without a repair attempt, after a short check by our team. After 14 days the normal warranty process applies.

## Urgent cases

If the item was a gift or you need it by a specific date, mention the date in your request. Replacements for DOA and transport damage are sent by express delivery where available.
